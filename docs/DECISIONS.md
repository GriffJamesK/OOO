## Architecture

- 2026-11-02: `Kit.Core` references `Unity.InputSystem` (a Unity package, not a project asmdef) so DebugOverlay can read the backquote key under New-Input-only.
- 2026-11-02: `Kit.Gameplay` also references `Unity.InputSystem`; it is needed to call `ReadValue`/`WasPressedThisFrame` on KitActions.
- 2026-11-02: Game-flow request events (`PlayRequested`, `RestartRequested`, `PauseToggleRequested`, `QuitRequested`) were added to `GameEvents` in Core so UI can drive Gameplay without referencing it.
- 2026-11-02: A shared `KitHost` creates one hidden DontDestroyOnLoad object for all self-installing runners (TimerRunner, TweenRunner, JuiceRunner, DebugOverlay). EventBus is a plain static class and needs no runner.
- 2026-11-02: Timer and Tween logic is split into pure classes (`TimerScheduler`, `TweenState`) so edit-mode tests step time by hand.
- 2026-11-02: `ObjectPool<T>` is generic over any class (factory + callbacks); `ObjectPool.ForPrefab` handles GameObject activation and the PooledBehaviour callbacks.
- 2026-11-02: `Juice.Punch` punches localScale. `Juice.Shake` offsets the camera between URP begin/end camera rendering, so it composes with Cinemachine. `HitStop` drops timeScale to 0.02 on real time.
- 2026-11-02: `StateMachine` publishes `StateChanged<TState>` and registers itself with `StateMachineRegistry` for the DebugOverlay; owners call `Dispose()`.
- 2026-11-02: Camera rigs: `CameraRig_Base` holds the real Camera (Brain, post-processing) and an empty CinemachineCamera. The variants add OrbitalFollow+RotationComposer (third person), HardLockToTarget+PanTilt (FPS), and Follow+RotationComposer (top-down). `CameraRig` drives the axes from `KitActions.Look`, so no CinemachineInputAxisController is used.
- 2026-11-02: The spec puts the active rig in Boot and also says only one rig is active "in Sandbox". Resolved by placing exactly one rig in Boot, which renders Sandbox when loaded additively. Swap the rig by replacing that prefab instance. *(Rig changed to FPS; see First-person and greybox defaults.)*
- 2026-11-02: Player prefabs: `Player_Base` (CharacterController 1.8 m with the pivot at the feet, a CameraTarget child at 1.6 m) and the `Player_Capsule` variant (body + facing nose). The GameManager spawns the player at `PlayerSpawn` into Sandbox, so a restart that unloads Sandbox removes it.
- 2026-11-02: Flow: Boot loads Sandbox additively and shows the title/main menu; Play hides the menu and enables input (GameStarted); Restart unloads and reloads Sandbox and respawns. Sandbox is set as the active scene so its lighting and fog apply.
- 2026-11-02: UI_Canvas contains its own EventSystem (InputSystemUIInputModule with default UI actions). The Pause menu has Resume as well as Restart/Quit.
- 2026-11-02: Panel transitions fade CanvasGroups with unscaled-time tweens so they animate while paused.
- 2026-11-02: Generated assets (materials, volumes, prefabs, scenes, build settings) come from `Editor/KitBuilder` (menu Kit > Rebuild Kit Assets) and are idempotent. *(Configs are now create-only; see below.)*

## Owner feedback: head bob, slide, chaining, speed FOV (2026-11-02)

- 2026-11-02: Head bob was too strong. Amplitude 0.045 -> 0.018 m, stride 1.6 -> 3.2 m per cycle (about 3.75 dips/s at 6 m/s, was about 7.5), sway 0.5 -> 0.35. The bob now fades in and out instead of snapping, and is off while sliding or airborne. Owner-requested, so the existing `PlayerConfig.asset` was updated too. Measured walking bob: about 3.6 cm peak to peak (was about 9 cm).
- 2026-11-02: New `Slide` action on the Player map: Left Ctrl, C, or gamepad B/East. B is also `UI/Cancel`, which is a separate map and unused in play.
- 2026-11-02: Movement split into a walking ramp (unchanged curves) plus a momentum layer. Above walking pace, velocity is steered (`GroundSteer` 360 deg/s, `AirSteer` 120 deg/s, `SlideSteer` 60 deg/s) rather than rebuilt each frame. When momentum drops back to walking pace, `SpeedRamp.SnapToFraction` hands off without a speed jump. Walls absorb momentum (speed can only shrink to what the CharacterController actually moved).
- 2026-11-02: Slide: starts on Slide press while grounded at 5 m/s or more (`SlideMinStartSpeed`). Adds `SlideBoost` 3 m/s, with no boost if another boosted slide started within 0.6 s. Friction 4 m/s^2; ends on release or below 3 m/s. Capsule drops to 1.0 m and the eye to 0.8 m. If there is no headroom to stand, the player stays low at `CrouchSpeed` 3 m/s until clear. Sliding off a ledge ends the slide and keeps the momentum in the air.
- 2026-11-02: Chaining: a jump is "chained" if it comes from a slide or within `ChainWindow` 0.15 s of landing. A chained jump keeps all horizontal speed and adds `HopBoost` 1 m/s. One landing grants one chained hop. A 0.12 s jump buffer makes landing hops fair. Holding Slide on landing starts a slide immediately (slide-hop-slide chains). No air drag; in the air you can only accelerate up to walking pace, so speed comes from chaining, not air-strafing.
- 2026-11-02: All boosts use `Momentum.ChainBoost`: full boost at walking pace, shrinking linearly to zero at `MaxSpeed` (18 m/s at the time; now 26, see below). Chaining always pays, but speed can't run away. After the chain window, ground friction is 14 m/s^2 down to walking pace. Measured in Sandbox: sprint 9 m/s -> 15.7 m/s after four slide-hops.
- 2026-11-02: FOV is now speed-based, replacing the sprint-only kick (`SprintFovKick` removed, `SpeedFovKick` 20 deg added; now 35, see below). FOV is 95 at walking pace and below, widening along `SpeedFovCurve` (ease-out) to the full kick at `MaxSpeed`. Sprinting (9 m/s) reads about +9 deg, similar to the old kick. `FovResponse` default 8/s (the existing asset keeps its tuned 10/s).
- 2026-11-02: Play-mode slide tests use a virtual gamepad. The Input System resets keyboards when the Editor is unfocused (and in batch mode), which made a keyboard-driven test read W as released.
- 2026-11-02: Sandbox has no out-of-bounds respawn. At chain speeds you can run off the 50 m edge and fall forever (BACKLOG).

## Owner feedback: slide-jump turning, escalating chains, stronger speed FOV (2026-11-02)

- 2026-11-02: A slide-jump snaps horizontal momentum toward the camera's flattened forward, up to `SlideJumpTurn` 120 deg, keeping speed. Instant rather than eased, because the request was a sharp turn. "Releasing the slide into the jump" counts too: letting go of Slide opens a `ChainWindow` (0.15 s) in which a Jump still counts as a slide-jump, and in which ground friction does not apply yet. Chained hops off a plain landing are not redirected; they keep their direction.
- 2026-11-02: Escalating chains: `ChainStreak` counts consecutive chained jumps, and each slide and hop boost is multiplied by 1 + 0.25 x streak (capped at 8 chains = 3x). The streak breaks when you stand on the ground outside the chain window. `MaxSpeed` raised 18 -> 26 m/s to give the build-up room. Measured in Sandbox, speed at each slide-jump: 9.0, 14.7, 17.7, 20.2, 22.1, 23.5, 24.5 m/s (it previously levelled off around 16). Per-jump gains still taper near the cap because `ChainBoost` keeps its headroom falloff, which stops speed running away.
- 2026-11-02: Speed FOV: `SpeedFovKick` 20 -> 35 deg over the larger walk-to-26 m/s range, with the same ease-out curve (sprint about +10, 15 m/s about +25). Added `BoostFovPunch`: +6 deg on every slide or hop boost, fading over 0.35 s, so each gain is felt. Measured peak in Sandbox: about 135 deg at 24.5 m/s with a punch. All of this is tunable in `PlayerConfig`. The owner's asset was updated (MaxSpeed 26, SpeedFovKick 35) because the owner asked for the change.
- 2026-11-02: Play-mode movement tests now wait until the controller is grounded (up to 2 s) instead of a fixed time, and sync physics after creating ground. A fixed 0.1 s wait failed in batch mode, where first frames are uneven.

- 2026-11-02: Limit tests are named for what they test: `Stairs_Rise30cm_Limit` (= CharacterController step offset; climbable), `Ramp_50deg_TooSteep` (past the 45 deg slope limit), curbs 0.1-0.75 m, vault boxes 0.5-2.5 m, jump gaps 2-5 m, maze tunnels at 1.25 m clearance (standing 1.8 m blocks, sliding 1.0 m fits).
- 2026-11-02: Course check in play mode: all three stair rises and the 15/30/44 deg ramps reach the 3 m tier; the 50 deg ramp can't be climbed. Curbs up to 0.35 m step up (the capsule's rounded bottom rides 5 cm past the 0.3 m step offset). Walking into a maze tunnel stops at the ceiling; sliding passes under it and stays crouched inside.
- 2026-11-02: Out-of-bounds respawn: `GameConfig.KillHeight` (-25 m). `GameManager` respawns the player at `PlayerSpawn` with all momentum cleared (`PlayerController.Respawn`) and republishes `PlayerSpawned` so the camera snaps. `PlayerDebug.Teleport` now uses the same path.
- 2026-11-02: Wall run: airborne, holding a direction, at 7 m/s or more, with a near-vertical wall within 0.45 m of the capsule. Probes go to both sides and the two front diagonals, so angled approaches are seen before contact. Approaches steeper than 75 deg off the wall line are treated as collisions. On attach, momentum turns along the wall and vertical speed is set to `WallRunUpSpeed` 3.5 under `WallRunGravity` -7: a gentle arc of about 0.9 m up, then a slow descent. Friction 1.5 m/s^2, max 1.4 s. Curved walls are followed by re-reading the wall normal each frame. The camera rolls 12 deg away from the wall (Cinemachine Dutch), driven by the player like FOV.
- 2026-11-02: A wall run ends on release, when the wall ends, below 0.6x the minimum speed, on landing, after the max time, or when input points sharply away (dot > 0.9, about 65 deg off the wall line). Looking around while holding forward must not knock you off; an earlier 0.5 threshold did.
- 2026-11-02: Wall jump: Jump on the wall, or within 0.15 s of leaving it. Launch direction = run direction turned up to 60 deg toward where you look (never into the wall), tilted away from the wall by `WallJumpPush`. Total speed = previous speed + `WallJumpBoost` 1.5 m/s (streak-scaled, cap-tapered), so the gain is exactly "a little". Height is 0.9x a normal jump. Air steering is locked for 0.25 s after the kick so it isn't immediately steered back parallel to the wall. It counts as a chain.
- 2026-11-02: Re-grab rule: the same wall (normal within 30 deg) can't be re-grabbed for 0.5 s; a different wall can, so corridor wall-to-wall chains work. Measured in the 6 m corridor: R, L, R, L catches with speed 9.0 -> 9.7 -> 10.7 -> 11.8 m/s. Each catch also gains about 1.3 m of height, so long chains climb (tunable: `WallRunUpSpeed`, `WallJumpUpFactor`).
- 2026-11-02: `KitInput` also releases its actions before every script reload and on exiting play mode (Editor only). The quit hook alone missed some reloads, and the leak assert came back.
- 2026-11-02: **Bug fix, collision absorption:** walls now remove only the into-wall part of horizontal velocity, using `OnControllerColliderHit` normals. The old check compared against `controller.velocity` and kept the lower value every frame, so at very high frame rates float noise ratcheted carried momentum down (batch mode at about 5000 fps bled a 9 m/s wall run to 0 in 0.3 s). This affected airborne momentum, slides and wall runs on any very-high-fps machine. Regression test: `AirMomentum_IsKept_AtVeryHighFrameRate` (forces 2000 fps with `Time.captureDeltaTime`).
- 2026-11-02: **Bug fix, frame-rate-dependent jump height:** the player's `CharacterController.minMoveDistance` is now 0 (set in `PlayerController.Awake` and on the prefab). At the default 1 mm, the controller dropped the vertical part of any move smaller than that, so at high frame rates slow vertical motion (jump apex, wall-run arc) was silently lost. At 5000 fps a 1.5 m jump peaked at 1.08 m and wall runs sagged to the ground. Unity's docs recommend 0. The same regression test now checks full jump height at 5000 fps; it fails with the old value.
- 2026-11-02: `KitBuilder.BuildAll` refuses to run in play mode. Run mid-play, it half-built: prefabs saved, scenes failed, and UIRouter.Awake threw on the half-built canvas.

## Owner request: easier air control, smooth stairs, surf ramp (2026-11-02)

- 2026-11-02: **Easy air control (owner request), partly reversing the pure Source air model:** holding forward in the air steers momentum toward the view at `AirSteer` (120 deg/s), and below walking pace you accelerate toward it (`AirWalkAcceleration` 12 m/s^2). Steering is scaled by the *forward* part of the input only. The first version steered on any input, and a pure strafe into a surf ramp swung momentum sideways and threw the player over the ridge (0 surf frames in a scripted run), so strafing keeps the Source-only behaviour.
- 2026-11-02: **Stairs regression and fix:** the phase-8 rule "grounded only if the best contact normal is walkable", plus clipping velocity on every non-walkable contact, made stairs clunky: the capsule's rounded bottom on a step edge reports a slanted normal, so the player went airborne on each step and risers clipped forward speed. Fix, for all stairs and ramps: a downward raycast from the feet confirms a walkable floor within step offset; riser contacts below step-offset height are not clipped while grounded; walking off a step snaps down up to `GroundSnapDistance` (0.45 m) when the previous frame was grounded and not jumping; the camera eases the per-frame step pop over `StepSmoothTime` (0.1 s) on flat ground only, so ramps don't lag. A play-mode test (12 steps of 0.25 m, up and down) needs >= 90% grounded frames and camera changes < 8 cm per frame; it failed before the fix (567 of 1925 frames grounded going up).
- 2026-11-02: **Surf physics:** with plain air settings (accelerate 12, gravity -30) a scripted strafe surfed only ~5 m before sliding off. Like Source surf servers (high `sv_airaccelerate`, gravity 800 vs the kit's weighty jumps), surfing uses `SurfAirAccelerate` 100 and `SurfGravityScale` 0.5, held for `SurfGrace` 0.15 s after losing ramp contact so brief gaps don't flicker. The same scripted strafe now rides the full 40 m ramp.
- 2026-11-02: **Surf ramp** in the test bay: a two-sided 60 deg ridge, 6 m high and 40 m long (x -18, z -15 to 25), DevOrange faces with a dark ridge cap; a 7 m start platform at its south end reached by stairs. 60 deg is well past the 45 deg slope limit, close to typical Source surf ramps.

## Owner request: raised surf prism, leave surfaces the way you face (2026-11-02)

- 2026-11-02: **Surf ramp rebuilt** as one triangular prism mesh (`Generated/Mesh_SurfRamp.asset`, built by `KitBuilder.PrismMesh`, MeshCollider) instead of two overlapping rotated boxes, whose slabs crossed at the ridge and made the top uneven. - 2026-11-02: **Cause of the "pulled toward the ramp" feeling:** air steering (`AirSteer`) turned momentum toward the *input* direction. Surfing with W+A, that is a diagonal into the ramp, so after leaving the ramp you were steered sideways back toward it. It now turns toward the view direction. A play-mode test (fly off the end of a floating 60 deg ramp, check you head where you face) fails without the fix (35 deg of drift down the slope) and passes with it.
- 2026-11-02: **Leaving a surface goes the way you face (owner request):** leaving a surf ramp (detected by a short ray from the capsule's bottom sphere toward the ramp, so brief contact gaps don't count) or ending a wall run without jumping turns momentum up to `LeaveSurfaceTurn` (90 deg) toward the view, keeping speed, never back into the surface. This also drops the small push into the wall that a wall run carries. This is a deliberate departure from Source, where leaving a ramp keeps its velocity.
- 2026-11-02: **Wall jumps launch where you look:** `Momentum.WallJump` now uses the aim direction as-is when it clears the wall by at least the `WallJumpPush` angle; before, the push was always added on top, so you left further off the wall than you looked. `WallJumpAimTurn` went 60 -> 90 (default and the owner's `PlayerConfig.asset`) so looking straight off the wall works, by owner request.
- 2026-11-02: **Surf detection fix:** `IsSurfing` no longer requires `controller.isGrounded`. On the mesh ramp the controller reported the steep face as a side hit, so surfing (and the surf air-accelerate and gravity) dropped out mid-ramp. Any upward-facing contact steeper than the slope limit now counts.
